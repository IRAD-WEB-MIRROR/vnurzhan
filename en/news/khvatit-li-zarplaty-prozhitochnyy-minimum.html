Will your wage stretch? Work it out yourself

The Statistics Office has published the subsistence minimum for the third quarter: 1,040 som for a working-age adult. Vecherniy Nurzhan's calculator lets you set your own wage against it.

Azamat Beysheev13 November 2026, 18:30

The Territorial Statistics Office has published the subsistence minimum for the third quarter of 2026: 1,040 som a month for a working-age adult, 800 for a pensioner and 900 for a child, 960 som per person on average.

The subsistence minimum is the cost of a minimum set of food, goods and services for one person. The Office prices it every quarter from its own price survey; food makes up about 55 per cent.

From a wage the employer withholds 10 per cent income tax and 2 per cent for the State Pension Fund. Of the minimum wage of 1,000 som, 880 reaches the worker: less than the minimum for one adult. The minimum pension, 700 som, is below the pensioners' figure too.

The figures
Subsistence minimum, Q3 2026: working-age adult 1,040 som
Pensioner 800, child 900
Minimum wage after deductions: 880 som
Average Nurzhan wage after deductions: 3,432 som

The average Nurzhan wage of 3,900 som pays out 3,432 som: the subsistence minimum for three adults. A one-room flat in Nurzhan rents for 750–1,200 som a month.

Below is the editors' calculator. Enter your wage before deductions, say how many people live on it and whether you rent, and see what is left after the subsistence minimum. Its prices are updated every day.

We count rent separately: the subsistence minimum covers utilities but not the rent on a flat. The calculation is for guidance only: patents, benefits and a second wage in the family are not counted.
