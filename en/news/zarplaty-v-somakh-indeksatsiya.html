Wage indexing in Kyrgyzstan: what about pay here

The Kyrgyz president has signed a law on wage indexing. In Irad public-sector pay is still set in som, no indexation has been announced, and the 2027 draft budget goes to the Mejlis this month.

Azamat Beysheev13 November 2026, 11:00

On 10 November the Kyrgyz president signed a law amending the Labour Code on wage indexing. The announcement gives no details. It is a law of a neighbouring country; Irad has passed no law of its own.

Even so, the question is not a distant one for Nurzhan. Public-sector wages and pensions are paid in som at the official rate of 8.40 to the dollar. The average ISEZ wage in 2025 was 3,250 som a month (3,900 in Nurzhan), a teacher's about 2,300, and the minimum wage 1,000 som. The average pension is 1,250 som.

The Teachers' and Educators' Union, chaired by Rahat Osmon kyzy, has asked since June for public-sector pay to be indexed to take account of changes in the dollar rate. The union has repeated the request now.

The figures
Official rate: 8.40 som to the dollar
Average ISEZ wage, 2025: 3,250 som
Nurzhan: 3,900 som
Teacher: about 2,300 som
Minimum wage: 1,000 som
Average pension: 1,250 som

The Ministry of Economy's position has not changed. As the ministry's press secretary Bekbolot Sherov recalled, wages are set in som and paid in som; no indexation has been announced. IradOdin reported on how the discussion stands on Thursday, the Talas Herald today.

TSU economist Professor Raisa Akmatova believes that indexing supports the incomes of those who draw a wage, but does not change where the prices of imported goods come from, and it is the budget that pays for it.

The 2027 draft budget is due to go to the Mejlis in November. The editors will follow the debate and report if any decisions on pay appear in it.
